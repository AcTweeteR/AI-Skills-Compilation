# Project vision

AI Skills Compilation aims to become the most dependable public reference for discovering and evaluating AI
skills, agents, MCPs, prompts, frameworks, and related tools—not the largest catalog.

## What dependable means

- A first-time visitor can find a relevant review without learning the repository structure.
- A review separates documented evidence from claims, inference, and uncertainty.
- Compatibility, permissions, risk, and safe-use boundaries remain independently visible.
- Contributors can propose a source quickly without weakening the review standard.
- Maintainers can keep every accepted record current without relying on complex infrastructure.
- Project growth increases trust and usefulness rather than entry count alone.

## Long-term product principles

1. **Neutrality:** describe evidence and limitations without promotional ranking.
2. **Traceability:** keep primary sources and review dates close to each decision.
3. **Conservatism:** prefer `unknown` over unsupported compatibility or safety claims.
4. **Proportionality:** spend more review effort on entries with greater access or execution risk.
5. **Static-first maintenance:** use reviewable files and offline checks until scale proves another system necessary.
6. **Community clarity:** make contribution routes obvious while keeping acceptance criteria rigorous.

## How success is evaluated

Repository health should be judged by review quality, correction speed, contributor retention, navigation success,
and stale-record reduction. Stars can improve discoverability, but they are not evidence of catalog accuracy.
