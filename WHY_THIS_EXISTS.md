# Why this exists

AI development resources are increasingly distributed across repositories, marketplaces, articles, plugins, and
vendor documentation. Discovery is easy; informed comparison is not.

Common lists answer “what exists?” but often leave harder questions unresolved:

- Is the source an individual tool, an index, a marketplace, or secondary commentary?
- Is compatibility directly documented or merely plausible?
- Can the resource read files, execute commands, persist context, use credentials, or modify code?
- Does “approved” describe safe software or only a narrow read-only use?
- When was the evidence last checked, and what remains unknown?

This repository exists to make those distinctions explicit in a compact, reviewable format. It preserves useful
negative decisions as well as approvals, because knowing why a source was rejected prevents repeated research.

The project deliberately gives up exhaustive coverage. A smaller catalog can remain coherent, correctable, and
honest about uncertainty. That tradeoff is the foundation of its identity and the reason community growth must be
measured by trust, not volume.
