# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment regardless of age, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and documented behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's sensitive information.
- Accept correction, disclose uncertainty, and prioritize community safety.
- Keep security discussions within an appropriate private reporting channel.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, deliberate
misinformation, publication of private information, or promoting malware, stolen credentials, or destructive
activity.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit, reject,
remove, or temporarily lock contributions and participation that violate it, using a response proportionate to the
conduct and risk.

Report sensitive conduct concerns through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For a non-sensitive
moderation concern, open an issue with the minimum information needed for maintainers to respond. Do not publish
personal data or confidential evidence.
